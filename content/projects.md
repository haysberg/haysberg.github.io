## [Gazette](https://github.com/haysberg/gazette)

![Gazette logo](/photos/projects/gazette.png)

An RSS aggregator that pulls together the latest from independent and alternative left-wing media in France — around 45 sources, refreshed every 15 minutes.

The delivery is super optimized through compressed images, aggressive cache, light HTML. Works perfectly fine even on a slow 3G link or an old ADSL router.

## [KAY/O](https://github.com/haysberg/kayo)

![KAY/O logo](/photos/projects/kayo.png)

This Discord bot lets you subscribe to Valorant esport teams or tournaments, sending you a notification when a match begins, and another one when a match ends. It's written in Python and discord.py, pulling match schedules from the VLR.GG API.
